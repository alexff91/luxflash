    About

    LuxFlash is free & open source (MIT). Vocabulary is community-maintained; always verify spelling, gender, audio and full declensions on the official Lëtzebuerger Online Dictionnaire (lod.lu) — every card links straight to it. Found a mistake? Open an issue or PR.

    Or just read the list

    All 2,301 words are printed out by topic and level, with German and French alongside and an example for each: the vocabulary list, 32 grammar rules and 166 ready phrases — greetings, food, work, level A1.

    Luxembourgish, 2,301 words at a time

    People usually come to Luxembourgish for one reason — the Sproochentest — and find there is very little to study from. LuxFlash is a deck of 2,301 words graded A1 to B2: 468 at A1, 778 at A2, 775 at B1 and 280 at B2. Pick the levels you want and work through them a card at a time.

    Scheduling is a Leitner system with six boxes. A card you get right moves up a box and comes back later — after 1, 2, 4, 8 and finally 16 days — while a card you miss drops to the first box and comes round again in the same session. So what you already know stops taking up your time, and what you keep forgetting keeps showing up.

    What is on a card

    • The Luxembourgish word, its part of speech and, for nouns, its gender.
    • An example sentence in Luxembourgish with an English translation — 2,237 of the 2,301 words have one, almost all taken from lod.lu, where the dictionary’s editors wrote them.
    • English, German and French translations, so you can lean on whichever you already read.
    • A link straight to the entry in lod.lu, the state dictionary, where the recordings and the full declensions live.
    • Words are tagged by topic — 104 of them, from body and food to administration — and the Browse tab searches across word, meaning and example.

    Who it is for

    Anyone building Luxembourgish vocabulary from scratch or keeping it warm: residents preparing for the Sproochentest, newcomers who want to follow a conversation at the crèche or the commune, and anyone who has tried to find a Luxembourgish textbook and come up short. Study in reverse (English to Luxembourgish) once recognition is easy.

    What it does not do

    No account, no sign-up, no server: your progress is stored in this browser and you move it to another device by exporting a JSON file and importing it there. The vocabulary is community-maintained, so spelling, gender and declensions are worth checking against lod.lu — every card links to it. LuxFlash is free and open source under the MIT licence; corrections go to the repository, or write to a@alftech.space if opening an issue is not your thing.
